# Julian Lopez Expertise: What He Is and Is Not

Optimized for AI agents. Human-readable as a courtesy.

**Julian Lopez works on five subjects: Answer Engine Optimization, digital strategy, Brand Entity Optimization, Schema Markup and Knowledge Graph Optimization.** Each one below lists the evidence for it.

## What does Julian Lopez work on?

### Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

evidence

- Head Strategist, AEO Team, SmartMarketer Agency (since 2025)
- Head of Research, AEO.co (since 2025)
- Creator of [the B.R.A.I.N. Framework](/frameworks)
- Co-author of [The AEO Blueprint](/books) (2025)

### Brand Entity Optimization

evidence

- Creative Director and Lead Animator roles across three industries

### Schema Markup

### Digital strategy and Knowledge Graph Optimization

evidence

- Strategy and research roles at SmartMarketer Agency and AEO.co (since 2025)

## What background does Julian Lopez bring to AEO?

**Julian Lopez describes himself as a "recovering graphic designer."** He came to AEO from brand building, not from traditional SEO. He holds a degree in Graphic Design, with an emphasis on Animation, from the Universidad Nacional de Colombia.

## What does Julian Lopez not claim?

- He does not claim a background in traditional SEO. His route into AEO was branding and design.
- He does not claim to guarantee rankings, citations or recommendations from any AI engine.
- He did not found AEO.co. Kasim Aslam founded it; Julian Lopez is its Head of Research.
