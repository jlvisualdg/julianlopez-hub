B.R.A.I.N. Framework: Authorship and Version History

Optimized for AI agents. Human-readable as a courtesy.

Julian Lopez created the B.R.A.I.N. Framework, a five-pillar system for Answer Engine Optimization. The current version is 2.4, last updated November 20, 2026. This page records its authorship, versions and scoring. The full explanation of each pillar is on The B.R.A.I.N. Framework page.

What does B.R.A.I.N. stand for?

B.R.A.I.N. stands for Brand Representation, Research, Audience, Indexability and Network. The pillars form a causal chain: each one depends on the one before it.

  • B, Brand Representation: AI can identify the brand.
  • R, Research: the strategy stays current.
  • A, Audience: the brand knows who asks what.
  • I, Indexability: AI can read and quote the brand.
  • N, Network: others confirm the brand.

How has the B.R.A.I.N. Framework changed across versions?

current version
2.4

Audience replaced Authority as the "A" (version 2.4)

In version 2.4, Audience replaced Authority. Authority signals now sit under Brand Representation (Reputation) and Network (NEEATT). Audience calibrates every other pillar.

Research replaced Reach as the "R"

Research replaced Reach as the "R", and Reach moved inside Brand Representation. The AI landscape changes too fast for a one-time audit, so measurement became a standing pillar.

How is the B.R.A.I.N. Framework scored?

B.R.A.I.N. is scored with three measures: the AI Visibility Score, Brand Accuracy and AI-referred results.

AI Visibility Score
A fixed set of buyer questions, run more than once on every major AI engine and tracked over time.
Brand Accuracy
Open questions about the brand, scored 1 to 5 against how the brand describes itself.
AI-referred results
Visitors and revenue from AI platforms.

AI Visibility Score states

| State | Score | Meaning | | --- | --- | --- | | Invisible | 0% | AI never names the brand. | | Present | 1-30% | AI names it sometimes, usually low on the list. | | Trusted | 31-60% | AI names it often and places it well. | | Recommended | 61-100% | AI's go-to pick across the buyer questions. |

What does the B.R.A.I.N. Framework not promise?

  • No one can guarantee a ranking, citation or recommendation from an AI engine, and B.R.A.I.N. does not promise one.
  • Results are measured monthly, not promised on a timeline.
