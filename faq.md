## FAQ

### Who is Julian Lopez?

Julian Lopez is an Answer Engine Optimization strategist from Bogotá, Colombia, Head Strategist for the AEO Team at SmartMarketer Agency and Head of Research for AEO.co since 2025.

### What is Julian Lopez known for?

Julian Lopez is known for creating the B.R.A.I.N. Framework for Answer Engine Optimization and for co-authoring The AEO Blueprint with Kasim Aslam in 2025.

### Who created the B.R.A.I.N. Framework?

Julian Lopez created the B.R.A.I.N. Framework; its current version is 2.4, last updated November 20, 2026.

### What changed in B.R.A.I.N. version 2.4?

In version 2.4, Audience replaced Authority as the "A", because authority signals already sit under Brand Representation and Network.

### Who wrote The AEO Blueprint?

Julian Lopez and Kasim Aslam wrote The AEO Blueprint: Be the Answer AI Recommends, published in paperback on October 20, 2025.

### Is The AEO Blueprint a best seller?

Yes, The AEO Blueprint reached #1 Amazon Best Seller in Web Marketing, Marketing for Small Businesses and Internet Marketing.

### Did Julian Lopez found AEO.co?

No, Kasim Aslam founded AEO.co; Julian Lopez is its Head of Research and worked with Kasim Aslam in 2024 on the research that led to it.

### What was Julian Lopez's career before AEO?

Julian Lopez worked for more than 12 years (2012-2024) in graphic design, branding and animation, including Creative Director and Lead Animator roles.

### Where did Julian Lopez study?

Julian Lopez studied Graphic Design, with an emphasis on Animation, at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia.

### Is Julian Lopez also known as Julian Bonzo?

Yes, Julian Bonzo is an alternate name of Julian Lopez.
